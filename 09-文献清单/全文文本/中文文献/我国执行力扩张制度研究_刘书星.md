内容提要：当前，对我国执行力扩张制度的研究大多仅局限于执行力主观范围的扩张和依赖
于借鉴域外立法例的扩张程序构建。不过，这些研究没有涵盖我国执行力扩张的全部内涵。我国
的执行力扩张制度，应包括执行力扩张的实体内容和程序内容。其中执行力扩张的实体内容包括
执行力主观范围的扩张和客观范围的扩张。主观范围的扩张除了执行力向执行承担人、标的物占
有人、诉讼担当之被担当人的扩张外，还应包括基于执行担保的约定扩张、基于社会发展需要的政
策性扩张。执行力的客观范围的扩张包括执行标的的扩张和执行措施的扩张。对执行力扩张的
程序内容，应当根据扩张中是否发生了诉的标的的变化进行不同的设计。
关键词：执行力扩张主观范围扩张
客观范围扩张

Study on Chinese Execution Expansion of Legal Document
Liu Shuxing
Abstract:Studies on execution expansions of legal document in judicial practice are mostly
limited to expansions of subjective scope and foreign procedural laws. These studies did not cover
all the contents of execution expansions. Chinese execution expansion theory, should include
substantive contents and procedural contents. Substantive contents include expansion of subjective
scope of and expansion of objective scope. The objects of the former include guarantor based on
agreement expansion, the third party based on policy expansion, besides the successor of the
party of execution, the person who is in possession of the subject matter for the party of execution
or his successor, litigation undertaker. The latter include execution object expansions and execution
measure expansions. Procedural contents of execution expansion should be formed differently
according to diversification of the object of action.
Keywords :execution expansion of legal document;execution expansion of subjective scope;
execution expansion of objective scope;policy execution expansion
