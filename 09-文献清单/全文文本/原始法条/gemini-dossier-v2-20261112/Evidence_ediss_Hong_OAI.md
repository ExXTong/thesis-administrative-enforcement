- description:This work examines examples of the decontamination            duty of legal succession in police law. It deals with            the decontamination responsibilities of the legal            successors for the hazardous soil changes and the            clean-up of former waste deposits. Firstly, the            fundamentals of the legal succession are represented.            This should enable one to grasp the actual meaning and            conversion of the decontamination responsibility of            legal successors. The decontamination duty of the legal            successors is examined in the federal soil protection            act (in german called Bundes-Bodenschutzgesetz). Then            the specific questions of the legal succession are            explained in particular as it refers the concrete            arrangement of the responsibility. In addition, the            liabilities of the decontamination duty of the legal            successor are examined to indicate possible limits of            the legal successors. Finally, the constitutionality of            the decontamination duty of the legal successors is            mentioned. Therefore, this part deals with the            liability of the decontamination duty if it is against            the basic rights of the legal successors, and if the            decontamination duty of the legal successors offends            nonretoactivity under constitutional legal            principle.
