Docket Text
 MEMORANDUM & ORDER re 77, 97, and 108: For the reasons set forth in the attached, the Court grants in part and denies in part the Government's 77 and 97 motions for sanctions, and further denies Defendant's 108 motion for a stay pending her appeal. Discovery is closed. The final date to take the first step in dispositive motion practice shall be October 3, 2025. The parties shall comply with Judge Kovner's Individual Practices and Rules regarding dispositive motion practice. The Government is ordered to serve a copy of this order on Defendant and file proof of service on the docket by September 29, 2025. Ordered by Magistrate Judge Joseph A. Marutollo on 9/26/2025. (EG)
